Repair

Roof Repair

The need for a roof repair can come from any number of causes. Storm damage, overgrown trees, fallen trees, animals, and regular wear and tear are just a few reasons homeowners end up needing roof repair. Often homeowners only discover the problem when they see wet spots on their ceilings or walls after heavy rain. When you see signs like these, Wilmington Roofing Pros is the best place to call. We will be out quickly to provide honest, trustworthy roof repair to your home or business. We never try to talk you into more than you need.

Common Repair Causes

  • Storm and wind damage
  • Fallen or overgrown trees
  • Animal damage
  • Wear and tear over time
  • Roof leak & moisture intrusion
  • Damaged or missing shingles
